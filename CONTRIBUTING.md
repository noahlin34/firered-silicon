# Contributing

Thanks for your interest in FireRed Silicon!

Contributions and pull requests of all kinds are welcome. The project is experimental and personally maintained, so code, fixes, experiments, documentation, research, and ideas are all welcome.

## Review process

I will hand-review contributions on a case-by-case basis. At this time, there is no formal contribution framework, required contribution format, or set of code-quality requirements.

To set expectations clearly, I cannot guarantee that any contribution will be acknowledged, receive a response, be accepted, or be merged. Decisions will depend on the project's direction, compatibility with the current experiment, available time, and my judgment at the time of review.

A lack of response or a decision not to merge should not be interpreted as a judgment about the contributor or the effort involved. Thanks for helping explore what a native Apple Silicon port might look like.
